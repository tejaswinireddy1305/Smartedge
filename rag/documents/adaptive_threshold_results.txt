SmartEdge Adaptive Threshold Benchmark

The SmartEdge predictive caching system was evaluated using a NASA request trace containing 10,000 requests.

The experiment evaluated cache capacities of 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 300 and 500.

Multiple admission thresholds were evaluated. The best tested threshold was 0.1 across the evaluated capacities.

At cache capacity 25:
LRU hit rate: 31.66%
LFU hit rate: 44.45%
SmartEdge hit rate: 47.26%
SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 2.81 percentage points.
SmartEdge admissions: 2349.
SmartEdge evictions: 2324.

Overall observation:

SmartEdge performs particularly well at constrained cache capacities. Its advantage over LFU decreases as cache capacity increases.

The strongest improvement over LFU occurs at capacity 25, where SmartEdge achieves a 2.81 percentage point improvement.

At capacity 100, SmartEdge achieves 68.11% hit rate compared with 65.85% for LFU and 59.54% for LRU.

At capacity 500, SmartEdge and LFU are almost identical, with SmartEdge at 82.80% and LFU at 82.79%.

The experiments demonstrate that cache capacity and admission threshold have an important effect on SmartEdge performance.
