SmartEdge Benchmark Results

SmartEdge was evaluated against traditional LRU and LFU cache policies.

The adaptive threshold experiment used a NASA request trace with 10,000 requests.

At cache capacity 25:

LRU hit rate: 31.66%
LFU hit rate: 44.45%
SmartEdge hit rate: 47.26%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 2.81 percentage points.

At cache capacity 50:

LRU hit rate: 47.29%
LFU hit rate: 57.01%
SmartEdge hit rate: 59.30%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 2.29 percentage points.

At cache capacity 75:

LRU hit rate: 54.89%
LFU hit rate: 62.94%
SmartEdge hit rate: 64.61%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 1.67 percentage points.

At cache capacity 100:

LRU hit rate: 59.54%
LFU hit rate: 65.85%
SmartEdge hit rate: 68.11%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 2.26 percentage points.

At cache capacity 150:

LRU hit rate: 66.87%
LFU hit rate: 70.96%
SmartEdge hit rate: 72.10%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 1.14 percentage points.

At cache capacity 200:

LRU hit rate: 70.95%
LFU hit rate: 73.93%
SmartEdge hit rate: 75.14%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 1.21 percentage points.

At cache capacity 300:

LRU hit rate: 76.70%
LFU hit rate: 79.09%
SmartEdge hit rate: 79.34%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 0.25 percentage points.

At cache capacity 500:

LRU hit rate: 81.80%
LFU hit rate: 82.79%
SmartEdge hit rate: 82.80%

SmartEdge improvement over LFU: 0.01 percentage points.

The best tested threshold was 0.1 for the evaluated capacities.

The results indicate that SmartEdge performs particularly well under constrained cache capacities, while its advantage becomes smaller as cache capacity increases.
